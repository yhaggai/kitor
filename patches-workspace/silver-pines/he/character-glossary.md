# Silver Pines | Hebrew dialogue and character glossary

## Dialogue mood

The town should sound ordinary long enough for the wrong detail to land. Walker asks for directions, a name, a room. The stranger answers a slightly different question. Keep that gap. In the motel, let short questions and unfinished answers carry the fear. In the car, Alice and Eddie interrupt each other. Keep the fragments, repeated pleas, and changes of volume. In the storm, let Walker sound increasingly tired without giving him knowledge he does not yet have.

Write the investigation in conversational Israeli Hebrew. Walker says ״איפה אדי?״. Wulfe, interrogating the same man, says ״דבר איתי, אדי. איפה שמת את הגופה?״. The Driver’s television scenes allow punchier threats and exaggerated narration. Phone recordings and dispatchers use conventional service Hebrew. Keep the recurring phrase ״נסיעה אל הכוכבים״ recognizable even when the surrounding mood changes.

## Voice and address

The pronouns below describe **Hebrew grammatical treatment**, based on English dialogue, speaker keys and in-game lore. Where the text leaves gender open, use forms such as אפשר לעזור?, מה קרה?, שמעתי and תודה. A phone, station, or recording identifies a channel and may carry more than one voice. Keep hidden identities hidden until the English reveals them.
